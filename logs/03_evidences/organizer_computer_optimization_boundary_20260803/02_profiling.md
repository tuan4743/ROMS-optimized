# Stage 80 - organizer computer-optimization boundary: optimization impact

- This clarification authorizes consideration of lower-level communication and execution algorithms; it removes no performance evidence gate.
- Candidate changes must retain mathematical model/discretization and nested feedback semantics. Examples of potentially legal methods include communication aggregation, nonblocking/persistent messaging, packing/layout improvements, loop/thread restructuring and compiler/vectorization changes.
- Existing closed routes remain closed because their diagnostic performance evidence was negative or their dependency proof was absent, rather than because computer-level optimization was forbidden.
